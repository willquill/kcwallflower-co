About

The wallflower philosophy

KC Wallflower began with a love for old, well-made things and gardens that earn their keep. The name is a wink — the quiet one at the edge of the room who, up close, is the most interesting thing there.

Everything here is chosen by hand, one piece at a time, from estate sales and flea markets around Kansas City. We rent it out so those pieces can keep showing up at the moments that matter — and we help neighbors plant gardens that put flowers on the table and dinner on the plate.

— with love, from Kansas City
